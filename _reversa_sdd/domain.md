## 2. Core Business Rules

### 2.1. Clinical Invariants (`ClinicaPolicy`)
- **Age Range**: Patients must be between `0` (idade_min) and `120` (idade_max) years old.
- **Urgency Criteria**: Patients classified as **VERMELHO**, **LARANJA**, or **AMARELO** are considered urgent.
- **Staleness SLA**: Analytics data is considered stale (triggers Amber Alert) if it has not been updated for more than **2.0 hours**.
- **Commercial Month**: Fixed at **30.416 days** for throughput calculations.

### 2.2. Identity & Authorization (`DoctorProfile`)
- **Zero-Trust Principle**: Authentication (Keycloak) does NOT imply Authorization (CFM Validation).
- **CRM Validation**: A doctor is only authorized to view clinical data if `crm_verified = True`.
- **Fallback**: If CRM validation fails or is pending, access is denied (`IdentityContractViolationException`).

### 2.3. Ingestion Quality Gates
- **Circuit Breaker**: Disparado se a taxa de falhas exceder **5%** após o processamento de pelo menos **100 itens**.
- **DLQ (Dead Letter Queue)**: Payload inconsistente não interrompe o fluxo; é enviado para isolamento.
