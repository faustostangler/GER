# ADR-005: Business Policy Extraction from Infrastructure Config

> Generated by Reversa (Detective) on 2026-04-28
> Level: Detailed

## Context
Clinical business invariants (such as age ranges, clinical SLAs, and risk classifications) were previously coupled with infrastructure settings in the global environment files (`.env`). This allowed silent mutation of critical rules without tests or GitOps tracking.

## Decision
Extract all hospital domain invariants into an immutable `ClinicaPolicy` Value Object within the Core Domain (`src/domain/models.py`).

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Keep in `.env`**: High risk of drift and lack of validation.
2. **Database Persistence**: Added unnecessary I/O complexity for static business rules.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Guarantees consistency across the Bounded Context; prevents corruption in concurrent environments.
- **Negative**: Requires code changes to update default policy values (mitigated by infrastructure overrides).
